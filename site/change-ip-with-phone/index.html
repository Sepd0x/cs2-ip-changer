What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and admin rights: routes and firewall rules are system settings.
  • Windows Firewall turned on. Third-party firewalls that turn it off (some antivirus suites) leave the app without a kill switch, and it says so.
  • An Android phone with mobile data, and a USB cable or the phone's Wi-Fi hotspot.
  • USB debugging on the phone, only if you want to change IP from the PC.

Set it up

  1. Download CS2IPChanger.exe from the latest release into a folder only admins can change, like C:\Program Files\CS2 IP Changer. It runs as admin, so it doesn't run from Downloads: started there, it offers to install itself in that folder in one click. The exe isn't code-signed, so SmartScreen warns the first time: More info > Run anyway, or check the download first.
  2. Plug the phone in by USB, turn on USB tethering and turn off the phone's Wi-Fi, so it shares mobile data and not your home Wi-Fi. While the phone's Wi-Fi is on, the app keeps your servers and CS2 blocked.
  3. Run the app. Until a phone is set up it says Not protected yet and points you to Phone setup. Paste the servers you play on into Servers: IP:port, connect IP:port or steam://connect links (an IP without a port gets the Default port, 27015 unless you change it).
  4. Something missing? Phone setup under Phone checks the cable, the USB driver, USB debugging, tethering, the phone's Wi-Fi and where other apps go, does each step it can and tells you the one thing left.
  5. Open Connection. With CS2 and Steam running, every check should be green, including Phone IP is not your home IP. Run leak test to be sure.

On the hotspot instead of a cable? Connect the PC to it and pick it under Connection > How the PC connects to the phone.

Change IP from the PC

Turn on USB debugging on the phone and allow the PC when the phone asks. If ADB isn't installed, the app can download Google's platform-tools for you (about 7 MB).

Change IP then turns mobile data (or airplane mode) off and on until the carrier hands you a new IP, and keeps the old IPs in a list so you can see it changed. It also tells you if the new IP is in a new /16 range, because some admins ban whole ranges; Settings > Keep changing until the IP is in a new range keeps going until it is. On the hotspot, Change IP works if the phone is also plugged in by USB with USB debugging on.

Game only, whole PC or no phone

  • Game only: CS2, Steam and your servers use the phone. The browser, Discord and downloads stay on your home connection and don't eat mobile data.
  • Whole PC: everything goes through the phone, DNS included, and IPv6 is blocked so nothing leaves from home.
  • No phone: play on your home connection. Nothing is blocked, servers see your home IP, and the app says so. Accounts, Configs, Profiles and Regions keep working.

Once a phone has been connected, the kill switch (Block if the phone drops, in Checks) keeps your servers and CS2's game traffic on it: a pulled cable cuts CS2 off instead of falling back to your home IP. Before that, CS2 isn't locked, so trying the app without a phone never leaves CS2 unable to connect at home.

FAQ

Can Windows send only CS2 through my phone's hotspot?

Not by itself: when you tether a phone, Windows has no setting to pick one app for it. CS2 IP Changer adds the routes for CS2, Steam and your servers, so only they use the phone and the rest of the PC stays on your home internet. You can still send the whole PC through the phone if you want.

My carrier uses CGNAT. Is that a problem?

No. You share a public IP with other customers and servers see that shared one. Change IP still works, and when the carrier gives you the same IP back, the app keeps trying.

Will my ping be worse?

Usually, yes. Mobile data adds latency and jitter, so expect a higher ping than at home. A good 4G or 5G signal helps a lot: put the phone near a window and keep it charging. The ping in Servers is measured through the phone, so you see it before you join, and Connection quality tells you when the connection itself is weak. While it is weak, Whole PC drops to Game only so CS2 doesn't share the phone with the rest of the PC; turn off Fall back to Game only on a weak connection in Settings > Protection to stop that.

Does it work with a 4G or 5G USB dongle?

It works as the connection: if the app doesn't pick it on its own, choose it under Connection > How the PC connects to the phone. Change IP needs an Android phone with ADB, though.

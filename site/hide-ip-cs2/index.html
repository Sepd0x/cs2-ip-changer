Who sees your IP

  • Community server admins and their plugins. CS2 game traffic is UDP, sent straight between your PC and the server, and the server needs an address to send packets back. Whoever runs it can see and log that address, and their admin tools act on it.
  • Not official matchmaking. Valve's matchmaking goes through Steam Datagram Relay, so other players there don't get your IP.

What they can do with it

  • Ban it. An IP ban covers every account you play from that connection, and some admins ban a whole range (how bans work).
  • Place it. An IP shows your provider and a rough location.
  • Aim at it. An IP is what a DDoS hits. With your home IP, that's your home connection.

FAQ

Does official matchmaking show my IP?

Valve's matchmaking goes through Steam Datagram Relay, so other players don't get your IP there. Community servers you join directly are the ones that see it.

Can a community server admin see my IP?

Yes. The server needs your IP to send you game packets, so the admins and their plugins can see it, log it and ban it. With your phone's mobile data, what they get is your carrier's IP.

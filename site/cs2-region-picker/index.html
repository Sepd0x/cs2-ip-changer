How CS2 picks a server

CS2 matchmaking doesn't connect you straight to a server. It measures your ping to Valve's relays (Steam Datagram Relay) in each region and queues you where the ping is good. If a region's relays can't be reached, CS2 treats that region as unavailable and looks elsewhere.

That's what a CS2 server picker uses: it makes the regions you don't want unreachable, so the game never picks them.

What the Regions page does

  • The real list from Valve. The regions come from Valve's own relay list (GetSDRConfig, app 730), grouped by city and continent. The last list is saved, so the page works offline.
  • Ping for each region, measured the way the game will feel it: through the phone when you play on mobile data, through your home connection in No phone mode.
  • Shortcuts: Only this region, Closest 3, your continent only (Europe only, North America only and so on), Ping under X ms, and Allow all.
  • One firewall rule, for cs2.exe only. Turned-off regions are blocked on Valve's relay IPs only. Steam, your friends list and community servers are not touched.
  • Kept up to date. When Valve changes the relay list, the rule is rebuilt, so new relays in a region you turned off are blocked too.
  • One click back. Allow all removes the rule. So do --restore and tools/restore.cmd.

Things to know

  • Changes apply at once, and CS2 measures the regions again on its next start.
  • Blocking too much means long queues or no match at all, and Premier can take longer. Leave a few regions on.
  • It works with or without the phone. Regions don't depend on protection: they stay off until you click Allow all.

FAQ

How do I force CS2 to only put me in EU servers?

Open the Regions page and click your continent's shortcut (Europe only in Europe), or Only this region on one region. Every other region is blocked for cs2.exe, so matchmaking can only find you a match where you left regions on. Max Acceptable Matchmaking Ping in CS2's settings only limits the ping; it doesn't pick a region.

Does forcing a region get me banned?

The Regions page doesn't touch the game, its files or its memory. It adds one outbound Windows Firewall rule that stops cs2.exe from reaching Valve's relays in the regions you turned off, so to the game it looks like a bad connection to those relays. We can't speak for Valve and can't promise anything about how they treat it.

Does it change community servers or my friends list?

No. The rule only covers cs2.exe and only Valve's relay IPs. Community servers, Steam and friends work as before.

How do I undo it?

Click Allow all on the Regions page. Running the app with --restore, or tools/restore.cmd, also removes every rule the app made.

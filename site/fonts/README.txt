Fonts used by the site, served from here so visitors never load anything from Google.

funnel-sans.woff2     Funnel Sans, variable weight 300 to 800, Latin subset (body text)
fragment-mono.woff2   Fragment Mono Regular 400, Latin subset (labels, code, IPs)

If CS2's console shows NETWORK_DISCONNECT_STEAM_BANNED, that's the server's own ban list refusing your SteamID, not Steam or VAC. Many servers ban the IP and the account together.

Get back in, step by step

  1. Without any app: restarting the router sometimes gives you a new IP, if your provider hands out dynamic IPs, but it's still your home line. Playing on your phone's hotspot works too, since the server then sees the carrier's IP.
  2. With CS2 IP Changer: plug in the phone once (setup), and from then on Change IP and rejoin does it in one click: mobile data off and on over ADB, a new carrier IP, and back on the server. Ctrl+Shift+F10 does it from inside the game.
  3. The account too: after a ban the app logs into the next account that server hasn't banned, because the ban is often on the IP and the account.
  4. Range ban: the app tells you whether the new IP is in a new /16 and can keep trying until it is.
  5. Valve ban: when Steam or VAC refuses the account instead, the app says so and doesn't offer a new IP, because it wouldn't help.

What the app remembers

  • The account in use is marked as banned on that server and on the rest of the same community (the same tag in the server name, or a community name you set). New IP + next account skips it there, and joining with it asks first.
  • Your home IP never reaches the server, so it never lands on a ban list. No app can stop an admin from banning you; this keeps the ban off your house.

FAQ

Does CS2 IP ban?

Valve's bans (VAC bans, game bans and cooldowns) are on your Steam account, not your IP. Community servers are different: their admin plugins can ban a SteamID, an IP address or a whole IP range.

Will a new IP lift a VAC ban?

No. VAC and game bans are on the Steam account, not the IP, and nothing in CS2 IP Changer hides cheats from VAC or any other anti-cheat.

Some admins ban a whole IP range. Does that matter?

After each change the app tells you whether the new IP is in a new /16 or in the same one as before. If you want it to keep going until the range changes, turn on Settings > Keep changing until the IP is in a new range.

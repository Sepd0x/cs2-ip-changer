What you can do

  • Templates. Save a look (name, picture, real name, bio, country, privacy, custom URL) and apply it to any accounts.
  • Make the others look like one account in one click, or copy the look of any public Steam profile from its link.
  • Numbers and names: nightfall {n} gives nightfall 1, nightfall 2 and so on; {name} keeps each account's current name, so {name} | NV adds a tag.
  • Preview first. Each account is shown now and after. Apply changes them one after another with a pause, and each result is shown in plain words.
  • Undo. A copy of each profile, picture included, is kept before the first change. Restore original puts it back.
  • New accounts can get a template on their own the first time they show up.

Your login stays yours

Changing a profile needs a Steam web session, like your browser has. You sign in the way Steam's own login page works (password and Steam Guard, or a QR code from the Steam app). The password goes straight into Steam's encryption for that sign-in and is never saved. The app keeps only Steam's sign-in token, encrypted with Windows so only your Windows user can open it, and you can end it any time with Sign out on the Profiles page, or in Steam > Settings > Security > Authorized devices.

The app refuses a name, bio or URL that would show a login saved on your PC, and pictures are saved again as a new JPG, so photo data like GPS never reaches Steam.

FAQ

Can I change the name on all my Steam accounts at once?

Steam itself has no way to edit several accounts in one go. The Profiles page does it: pick the accounts, pick a template or one account's look, check the preview and click Apply. The accounts are changed one after another, and Restore original puts each one back.

Can it clear "previously known as"?

Yes, with Clear name history, which is off unless you tick it. That one is permanent: Steam has no way back.

Where are my templates kept?

On your PC only, in SRT_Dados/profiles. Nothing leaves your PC except what you apply to Steam.
